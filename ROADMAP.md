## Design doctrine (2026-07)

Two-tone surface: **Night ink `#0D1321` + Signal Gold `#F0B429`** on the
cool light ground. Complementary colours (green success, red noise,
violet drift, teal) appear only as *slight, small feature highlights* —
a check state, a data band, a status dot — never as washes, section
themes, or per-tab accents.
