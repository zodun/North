# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Young people (roughly 18–30) in the Caribbean — Jamaica is the launch market — who
feel directionless and want a calmer, more intentional alternative to
attention-optimized social feeds. They are mobile-first (the web app ships as a
PWA), often on mid-range Android over variable networks. Their context when they
open North is the opposite of killing time: they want to find direction — to
convert scattered inspiration into a few completed actions and to see honest
patterns in their own behavior. Secondary audiences: internal content/opportunity
curators (the original admin surface) and, later, opportunity providers posting
jobs, scholarships, and programmes.

## Product Purpose

North helps people convert inspiration into consistent, aligned action. Where
mainstream consumer apps optimize for attention, North optimizes for **direction**.
It runs as a single loop across five tabs:

- **For You** — a calm discovery feed surfacing purpose-, career-, and
  growth-oriented content (not a hyperstimulating one).
- **Mission** — turns inspiration into small, completed actions: a monthly goal
  broken into 4 weekly milestones and one daily micro-step, with a daily/weekly
  cadence the user chooses.
- **Signal** — reflects real behavior back to the user (a 0–100 Direction Score,
  active days, follow-through, focus match) plus a type-or-voice journal that
  separates the day's signal from its noise.
- **Opportunities** — converts clarity into concrete movement: jobs, internships,
  scholarships, accelerators, grants, ranked to the user's focus areas and
  preferred opportunity types.
- **Profile** — visualizes identity evolution over time (streaks, consistency,
  the active goal) so the user can feel themselves changing.

Success is not time-in-app. It is sustained mission completion and a measurable
lift in self-reported life alignment from onboarding to week 4.

## Brand Personality

**Clear · Premium · Directional.** Light, airy ("Soft Sky"), and considered; crafted
rather than cheap; always pointing somewhere. The voice is composed and honest — it
builds momentum without hype or pressure, and the Signal layer tells the truth rather
than flattering. Calm and clarity are the undertone (North is the antidote to
hyperstimulating feeds), but the surface reads premium and deliberate, never loud.

## Anti-references

- **Generic AI-app skin** (primary): purple-gradient-on-glass, blur everywhere, the
  interchangeable 2025/26 AI-wrapper look. North must never read as a template.
- **Attention-optimized social feeds** (foundational to the thesis): TikTok /
  Instagram infinite scroll, autoplay, dopamine-tuning. North inverts this — calm
  and cinematic, not engineered for dwell time.
- **Gamified-hustle pressure**: neon streak-guilt, leaderboard anxiety, badges that
  shame. Momentum should feel earned and quiet.
- **Cold corporate-SaaS dashboard**: KPI-card grids and dense enterprise data-viz
  that would strip the calm, human feel out of the Signal and Profile surfaces.

## Design Principles

1. **Direction over attention.** Every screen helps the user move, reflect, or
   decide — never just consume. If a pattern exists to maximize dwell time, cut it.
2. **Honest mirror.** Signal and Profile reflect real behavior plainly — no vanity
   metrics, no flattery. Trust comes from telling the truth calmly.
3. **Calm, cinematic restraint.** Dark, filmic, deliberate. Low-stimulation by
   default; color and motion are accents that earn their place, not constant noise.
   Premium through restraint, not decoration.
4. **One small step.** Reduce ambition to the next concrete action — today's
   micro-step, one opportunity, one message. The product's job is to make the next
   move obvious and doable.
5. **Built for the launch market.** Mobile-first PWA tuned for mid-range Android on
   variable Caribbean networks: fast, light, accessible, and locally relevant.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target **WCAG 2.1 AA**: ≥4.5:1 contrast for body text (≥3:1 for large/bold),
visible focus states, correct ARIA and keyboard order — already enforced in CI via
Biome's a11y rules. Every animation ships a `prefers-reduced-motion` alternative
(crossfade or instant). Performance and data-weight are treated as accessibility
concerns for this audience: optimize for mid-range Android over variable networks.
Behavioral data belongs to the user and is exportable/deletable, governed by the
Jamaica Data Protection Act 2020.
