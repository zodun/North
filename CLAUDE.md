# CLAUDE.md

## Design Context

Before any frontend/design work, read **[PRODUCT.md](PRODUCT.md)** (strategy) and
**[DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md)** (visual system). They are the source of truth for the
impeccable design skill and for staying on-brand.

- **Register:** `product` — the 5-tab app loop (For You · Mission · Opportunities ·
  Journal · Community; see ROADMAP.md). Design serves the workflow.
- **North Star:** *Soft Sky* — a calm, airy LIGHT field (daylight, not darkness); a
  gold needle that always points somewhere. Direction over attention.
- **Principles:** direction over attention · honest mirror · calm restraint · one
  small step · built for the launch market (mobile-first PWA, mid-range Android).
- **Palette (two-tone + slight highlights):** the surface is TWO tones — ink/Night
  `#0D1321` on cool sky `#F3F5F7` (white cards), with Signal Gold `#F0B429` as the
  single accent (= the next action; `#8A6400` as gold text/icons, ≥4.5:1). All other
  hues (green `#27AE60` success, red `#EB5757` noise, violet `#6C5CE7` drift, teal
  `#16A085`) are *slight, small feature highlights only* — a check state, a data
  band, a status dot — never washes, section themes, or per-tab accents. Night is
  also the splash ground and app-icon tile.
  Type: Plus Jakarta Sans (display, black weight) + Geist (body).
- **Tabs (2026-07 restructure):** For You · Mission · Opportunities · Journal ·
  Community. Signal and Profile live in the drawer.
- **Hard nos:** generic AI-app skin (purple-gradient-on-glass), the muddy AI-cream
  light default (sand/beige/parchment near-whites — North's light is a clean cool
  sky), attention-optimized social-feed patterns, gamified-hustle pressure,
  corporate-SaaS dashboards, `box-shadow` on cards, colored stripe
  `border-left/right` > 1px.
