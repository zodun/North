# CLAUDE.md

## Design Context

Before any frontend/design work, read **[PRODUCT.md](PRODUCT.md)** (strategy) and
**[DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md)** (visual system). They are the source of truth for the
impeccable design skill and for staying on-brand.

- **Register:** `product` — the 5-tab app loop (For You · Mission · Signal ·
  Opportunities · Profile). Design serves the workflow.
- **North Star:** *The Night Compass* — dark, cinematic; a gold needle that always
  points somewhere. Direction over attention.
- **Principles:** direction over attention · honest mirror · calm cinematic restraint
  · one small step · built for the launch market (mobile-first PWA, mid-range Android).
- **Palette (rationed to three meanings):** Signal Gold `#F5C842` = next action,
  Direction Teal `#3ECFBF` = on-course, Drift Violet `#7B61FF` = drift, on Night
  `#05050E`. Type: Plus Jakarta Sans (display, black weight) + Geist (body).
- **Hard nos:** generic AI-app skin (purple-gradient-on-glass), attention-optimized
  social-feed patterns, gamified-hustle pressure, corporate-SaaS dashboards,
  `box-shadow` on cards, colored stripe `border-left/right` > 1px.
